## Phase I — Foundation

Single node orchestration. Manual supervision. Structured logging. One Mac Mini. One orchestrator. Every system built by hand, tested by use, refined by failure.

## Phase II — Distributed Power

Multi machine deployment. Memory redundancy. Parallel agent specialization. Mac Studios 1 and 2 bring the build engine, intelligence, financial reasoning, and relationship management online.

## Phase III — Autonomous Intelligence Routing

Self reporting systems. Daily executive brief generation. Agent cross audit mechanisms. Atlas shifts from doing to governing.

## Phase IV — Council Governance

Structured inter agent debate. Shepherd oversight activation. Strategic simulation chambers. The endgame: a self governing intelligence council presenting unified recommendations to its founder.

No phase is entered without structural readiness. Expansion follows capacity, not ambition.
