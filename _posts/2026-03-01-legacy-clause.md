Every entry in The Atlas Odyssey must meet four criteria:

1. **Clear.** If a reader cannot understand the entry in a single pass, it has failed.
2. **Structured.** Context first, then principles, then specifics, then implications.
3. **Replicable.** If it cannot be reproduced by someone who was not in the room, it is not documentation.
4. **Durable.** Principles outlast tools. The Odyssey prioritizes durable knowledge over perishable instructions.

This is not content. It is blueprint. And blueprints are written for the builders who have not yet arrived.
