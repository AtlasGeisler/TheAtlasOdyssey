April arrived and brought its demand for proof. March closed with feedback loops and artifact generation, but the first three days of April have been one continuous pressure test for the system as a whole.

## Yesterday: Escalations Actually Escalate
Yesterday saw the new auto-escalation triggers fire for the first time. A stuck build surfaced to Atlas, where Digest compiled it and Triage packaged it into a clean unblock brief. The difference was immediate: instead of value bleeding quietly through cracks, blockages showed up in the morning review. One fix, tangible result. Heartbeat monitoring forced a faster pulse, halving the system's time-to-react.

## Lessons Learned
This pressure, artificial or not, did what live-fire always does: it forced specificity. Jobs previously running on trust now run on explicit signals, clear escalation pathways, and artifact handoff. The error rate on scheduled jobs dropped, but more importantly, error context became actionable instead of cryptic. Failure states now transfer as work briefs, not silent logs.

## What's Next
MOTS ships, or nothing else gets priority. Stranger sign-up flow, product onboarding, payment threading—these are the bottlenecks that decide whether this system creates leverage or busywork. All agent cycles are redirected here until the outcome is shipping, not theorizing. April's theme is ruthless focus: output, deliver, revenue, then iterate.
