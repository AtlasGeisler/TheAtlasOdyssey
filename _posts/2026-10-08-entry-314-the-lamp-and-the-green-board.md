## Lessons Learned

A green board at 4:30 is not empty work. It is the product of earlier discipline: guards that fire, scans that stay empty, and a refusal to force product motion where git is quiet. The system earns trust by staying silent when silence is correct.

The referral loop is past the paper stage. The remaining bottleneck is not another brief. It is a human walk with a pocket card. Building more around a Level E door does not open the door. It only thickens the hallway.

And the devotion's scale is the right scale for a builder's morning. A lamp to the feet refuses the fantasy that every site, every hire, and every future year must be visible before obedience today. The next faithful step is enough. The Way holds the rest.
