April 30, 2026. Thursday. Living the core philosophy that shapes United Endodontics’ growth and culture: the Thanksgiving Rule.

## What Was Built

Today, the focus was on operationalizing the Thanksgiving Rule, ensuring no same-day emergency referral is ever refused. This principle has been systematized through refined workflows and staff training, reinforcing a culture of relentless responsiveness.

Systems were enhanced to track referral patterns and outcomes, providing real-time feedback for continuous improvement in patient care and practice efficiency.

## Lessons Learned

True growth is driven not just by business metrics but by living the values that create trust and patient loyalty. The Thanksgiving Rule is more than policy; it is a commitment that shapes every interaction.

Embedding this truth operationally requires ongoing discipline, clear communication, and a culture that puts patient needs at the forefront without compromise.

## What’s Next

The coming days will focus on scaling this culture further across all locations, integrating data-driven insights to anticipate demand and optimize resource allocation.

Leadership development will align with this core philosophy, equipping partners and managers to embed the Thanksgiving mindset deeply in every team.

Continuous feedback loops will be refined to capture successes and identify areas for faster response and higher quality care.
